Who Am I?

Hi there! I'm Kai, the person behind Magic Dippy Egg Online. This website is my creative space where I bring various digital ideas to life. I have a passion for experimenting with technology, building things online, and exploring the world of retro computing.

I'm always curious about how things work and love diving into new projects, whether it's developing a game, coding a website, or tinkering with old computers. This site is a reflection of that curiosity and my journey in the digital realm.

What I Do

My interests are quite varied, but they generally revolve around creating and exploring with technology.

  • Game Development: I enjoy the process of bringing game ideas to life, from concept to code.
  • Web Development: Building websites, like this one, and experimenting with different web technologies is something I find rewarding.
  • Tech and Retro PCs: I have a fascination with computers, both modern and old. Exploring retro PCs is a particular interest.
